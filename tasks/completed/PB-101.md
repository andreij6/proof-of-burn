# PB-101 — Custom domain (optional)

Serve the SPA under a custom domain.

## Scope
- DNS (CNAME/TXT/ACME), `.well-known/ic-domains` in `dist/`, register/validate via the boundary-node REST API; configure `HttpAgent` host.

## Acceptance criteria
- App reachable over HTTPS at the custom domain with valid TLS.

## Notes / pitfalls
- Ensure `.ic-assets.json5` includes the `.well-known` directory.
