# PB-120 — Admin toggle not visible; surface the real app principal

Adding `vx7jq-…-xqe` to `admins` did not reveal the in-app admin control, because
**Internet Identity derives a different principal per dapp**. The principal you
log into *this* app with is not `vx7jq` (that came from another context), so the
`isAdmin` check (login principal ∈ admins) failed.

## Fix
- The header wallet pill is now a **click-to-copy** of your full app principal
  (works on mainnet, not just the local dev sidebar). Copy it, then add it:
  `icp canister call backend add_admin '(principal "<app-principal>")' -e production`.
- Once connectivity (PB-119) works + your real app principal is an admin, the
  "Admin · voting threshold" card appears.

## Note
`admin_set_default_threshold` also works from the CLI under any admin identity
(e.g. the deployer `xpvre-…`), independent of the browser login.
