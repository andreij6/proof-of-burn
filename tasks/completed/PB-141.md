# PB-141 — Leader card "+ X Pooled Voting Power" + syndicate total

Keep the leader neuron card exactly as it is today and append the pooled VP.
Plan §7 ("Leader neuron card").

## Frontend
- Fetch `get_pool_info` on load/refresh.
- On the existing leader card, append a single line:
  `+ {fmtVP(pooledVotingPower)} Pooled Voting Power` (sum of Active pool neurons).

- Header/tagline "total voting power" becomes **Total Syndicate VP** =
  leader VP + pooled VP.
- No other change to the leader card layout/content.
