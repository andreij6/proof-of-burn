# PB-102 — Monitoring & alerting

Operational visibility for a canister holding value.

## Scope
- Cycle-balance and freezing-threshold alerts; burn/refund audit-log export; error-rate monitoring on outcalls/inter-canister calls.

## Acceptance criteria
- Alerts fire on low cycles and on burn/refund anomalies; audit log queryable.

## Notes / pitfalls
- High-value canister: monitor and top up proactively rather than reactively.
