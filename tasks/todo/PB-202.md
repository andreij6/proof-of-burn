# PB-202 — Deck RNG (commit-reveal) + 7-card hand evaluator

Plan: `plans/poker/` (see README.md for locked decisions D1–D17).

## Scope (plans/poker/02)
- Fisher-Yates over a 32-byte `raw_rand` seed; `seed_hash` committed in the
  HandRecord before dealing; seed revealed at settle.
- Pure 7-card evaluator -> packed u32 strength (no external crates).
- Slow reference evaluator in tests for cross-checking.

## Acceptance (doc 09 Layer 1)
- Golden corpus (wheel, steel wheel, board plays, kicker chains, ties).
- Differential vs the PB-224 reference evaluator: 10k random boards in CI,
  100k as the milestone gate, zero disagreements; total-order check.
- Deck replay from revealed seed reproduces every deal byte-for-byte.
