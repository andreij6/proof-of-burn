# PB-143 — Join-pool setup wizard (verify before pay; draft resume)

The multi-step wizard launched from the sidebar Follow button. Verification
happens before any payment; the fee is the final step; abandoning keeps a Draft.
Plan §7 ("Setup wizard").

## Frontend
- **Step 1 — Intro:** what pooling is, the live initiation fee
  (`get_config().pool_initiation_fee_e8s`), and that payment is last.
- **Step 2 — Configure & verify (free):** instructions to (a) add the backend
  canister principal as a hotkey [show + copy], (b) follow leader #`<id>` on the
  Governance topic [NNS dapp link]. Enter `neuron_id` → **[Verify]** calls
  `create_pool_draft`. On success advance; on failure show which check failed
  (`HOTKEY_MISSING` / `NOT_FOLLOWING` / `ALREADY_REGISTERED`) and stay.
- **Step 3 — Pay (final):** reachable only with a Draft. Show
  `get_registration_address` + amount (`fee + 0.0003 ICP`). User funds it, then
  **[Finalize]** calls `finalize_pool_registration`. On success → Active, close,
  refresh sidebar.
- **Abandon-safe:** closing keeps the Draft (`get_my_pool_neuron`); "Resume setup"
  re-enters at step 3. "Discard draft" → `cancel_pool_draft` (+
  `refund_registration` if funded).

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] No payment is possible before a Draft exists (step 3 gated on a verified
      Draft).
- [ ] Verify failures display the specific reason and keep the user on step 2.
- [ ] Funding + Finalize produces an Active neuron and updates the sidebar/leader
      card without a reload.
- [ ] Closing mid-wizard preserves the Draft; reopening shows "Resume setup" and
      lands on step 3.
- [ ] "Discard draft" removes the Draft and (when funded) refunds the escrow.
- [ ] `npx tsc --noEmit --skipLibCheck` clean.
