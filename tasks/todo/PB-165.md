# PB-165 — Frontend: canvas renderer, slingshot input, HUD

The look and feel of the reference game, in our design system. Plan §4.

## Frontend
- `src/frontend/src/golf/renderer.ts` + `GolfCanvas` component: top-down
  hole rendering (green, sand, water, walls, cup, flag) using design-system
  tokens only (char surfaces, sprout green, haze sand, burn accent); the
  simulation animates by interpolating the PB-164 step iterator at display
  refresh rate (floats allowed here only).
- Slingshot input: pointer-down on/near the ball, drag away to aim — shot
  fires opposite the drag; live aim line + tick-marked power bar; drag
  distance → power (clamped 0..=1000); cancel by releasing at near-zero
  power. Pointer events (mouse + touch); canvas scales responsively
  (min 320 px wide).
- HUD: hole intro card (hole #, par), stroke counter, running scorecard
  strip with par-relative coloring (birdie sprout, bogey ember), water
  "+1 penalty" toast, pickup notice at the 12-stroke cap.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Plays a local free-play course end-to-end with mouse AND touch.
- [ ] No Miniclip-derived art, names, or layouts anywhere.
- [ ] All colors come from `index.css` tokens; no hardcoded hex.
- [ ] Aim/power quantization matches what is submitted on-chain
      (the rendered shot is the recorded shot).
