# PB-042 — Execute burn on threshold met

When a proposal reaches threshold by its deadline, burn the escrowed ICP.

## Scope
- For each `Pending` commitment, transfer escrowed ICP **to the ICP minting account** with `fee = 0` (this is the on-chain burn).
- Mark commitments `Burned`, record block indexes to the audit log and `VoteRecord` ([[PB-032]]); then trigger vote ([[PB-033]]) and social posts ([[PB-061]]).
- Batch/iterate safely across `await` boundaries.

## Acceptance criteria
- Escrowed totals are burned; ledger shows transfers to minting account; commitments marked `Burned`.
- Burned e8s recorded per proposal and per principal (Tier-3 dashboard).

## Notes / pitfalls
- Burn = transfer TO minting account, **fee = 0** (passing the normal fee fails `BadFee{expected_fee=0}`).
- Critical mutations before the `await`; release locks in cleanup ([[PB-045]]).
- Confirm the exact minting-account destination (DECISION in README).
