# PB-032 — Vote history log

Maintain and serve the neuron's past votes with outcome and ICP burned per vote (Tier 1+).

## Scope
- On each cast vote ([[PB-033]]) write a `VoteRecord` (proposal, vote, icp_burned_e8s, outcome, decided_at).
- Backfill from Governance ballots/proposal results where available.
- `list_vote_history() -> Vec<VoteRecord>` query.

## Acceptance criteria
- Vote history view ([[PB-075]]) shows proposal title, vote, ICP burned, date.
- Burned amount matches the burn executed in [[PB-042]].

## Notes / pitfalls
- Source of truth for "burned" is the ledger burn block index, not the committed total.
