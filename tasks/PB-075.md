# PB-075 — Vote history view (Tier 1+)

Tabular/card log of the neuron's past votes.

## Scope
- Each entry: proposal title, vote (yes/no/abstain), ICP burned, date — from [[PB-032]].

## Design source
Recreate `VoteHistory` (`design-system/project/pob-parts.jsx`): hairline-separated rows of title + vote `Chip` (`ok` yes / `muted` against) + mono burned amount in `--burn`, under the `list`-icon "Vote history" header. Gated for anon via `Gate` ("Sign in to unlock"); unlocks at Tier 1.

## Acceptance criteria
- Unlocks at Tier 1; renders all past votes with correct burned amounts.

## Notes / pitfalls
- "ICP burned" comes from the ledger burn record, not committed totals.
