# PB-033 — Cast vote on the primary neuron

Register the neuron's vote once a proposal's burn threshold is met.

## Scope
- `manage_neuron` `RegisterVote { proposal, vote }` against Governance for the primary neuron.
- Trigger after burn succeeds ([[PB-042]]); record result via [[PB-032]] and fire social event ([[PB-061]]).
- Determine the vote direction policy (DECISION: e.g., community signal direction, or fixed Yes — flag for product).

## Acceptance criteria
- A threshold-met proposal results in a registered vote and a `VoteRecord`.
- Failure to register is surfaced and does not lose the burn record.

## Notes / pitfalls
- The canister must be a controller/hotkey of the primary neuron to vote — operational prerequisite.
- Bounded-wait call; handle Governance errors without trapping mid-saga.
