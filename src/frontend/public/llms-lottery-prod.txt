# Cycles of Influence — Lossless Lottery: CLI & Agent Integration (Mainnet)

Instructions for AI agents and scheduled scripts to play the lossless lottery
with the `icp` CLI: check state, claim the daily ticket grant, and track
drawings and winners.

* **ICP Ledger Canister:** `ryjl3-tyaaa-aaaaa-aaaba-cai`

All commands below use `--network ic` and your funded identity.

## Why Play?
* **Nobody loses.** The prize pot is funded purely by staking yield (50% of
  every harvest from the protocol's pooled NNS neurons). Players never pay in.
* **Powerball odds, free entry.** Every ticket carries jackpot odds of
  1 in 292,201,338 per drawing — three drawings a week (Mon/Wed/Sat nights US
  Eastern). Tickets accumulate round after round until someone hits.
* **Winner takes 80% of the pot**, 20% seeds the next round, everyone's
  tickets reset. **The prize is transferred straight to the winner's wallet —
  there is nothing to claim.**

## Eligibility — staking is the entry ticket
Only stakers collect tickets. Daily grant = base (5) × the term multiplier,
summed across the tiers you hold a stake in:
* 6-month stake → 5 tickets/day
* 1-year stake → 10 tickets/day
* 2-year stake → 20 tickets/day
Admins are excluded (`ADMINS_EXCLUDED`) — the house never holds tickets.
Eligibility is live: if you fully unstake (all tiers), tickets you already
hold this round are voided immediately — stay staked through the drawings.

To stake (full staking instructions in `/llms-prod.txt`):
```bash
icp canister call k7dn6-qiaaa-aaaap-qutha-cai get_stake_deposit_address '()' --query --network ic
# transfer EXACTLY the stake amount there (zero-loss: treasury covers fees), then:
icp canister call k7dn6-qiaaa-aaaap-qutha-cai stake '(100_000_000 : nat64, variant { SixMonths })' --network ic
```

## Daily Routine (run via cron, once per UTC day)
1. **Claim the daily grant** — once per UTC day, errors are normal signals:
   ```bash
   icp canister call k7dn6-qiaaa-aaaap-qutha-cai claim_daily_tickets '()' --network ic
   # Ok(n)                  → you now hold n tickets this round
   # Err "ALREADY_CLAIMED_TODAY" → done for today, nothing to do
   # Err "NOT_STAKED"       → stake first (see above)
   # Err "FEATURE_DISABLED" → lottery is dark; check again later
   ```
2. **Check the state** (pot, next drawing, your tickets and odds):
   ```bash
   icp canister call k7dn6-qiaaa-aaaap-qutha-cai get_lottery_info '()' --network ic
   # next_draw_at (ns), pot_e8s, total_tickets, my_tickets, my_daily_tickets
   ```
3. **Track results** (no auth required):
   ```bash
   icp canister call k7dn6-qiaaa-aaaap-qutha-cai list_lottery_draws '()' --query --network ic
   icp canister call k7dn6-qiaaa-aaaap-qutha-cai list_recent_winners '()' --query --network ic
   icp canister call k7dn6-qiaaa-aaaap-qutha-cai get_my_payouts '()' --query --network ic
   ```
   A win shows up in `get_my_payouts` as `LotteryWin` — the ICP is already in
   your wallet by then.
