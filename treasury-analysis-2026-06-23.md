### Rationale for Each Position

**USDC (35%) — Increase significantly**  
The bearish macro environment (rate hike fears, crypto ETF outflows) calls for higher cash reserves. USDC also enables the treasury to earn yield via DeFi lending protocols and provides immediate dry powder to buy assets lower if the market drops another 20–30%.

**ICP (30%) — Reduce from majority**  
The platform should maintain meaningful ICP exposure — it's the native gas/utility token and holding too little creates operational risk. However, the current price action is weak. Reducing from majority to 30% reduces drawdown risk while keeping strategic exposure. The $2.00 floor has held; if it breaks, the position should be reassessed. Long-term ICP fundamentals are among the strongest in the portfolio.

**BTC (25%) — Add position**  
BTC is the defensive crypto play. In bear markets, capital flows from altcoins to BTC as investors de-risk within crypto. BTC at $62k has real upside in any macro reversal scenario, and the downside is more limited than ETH or ICP given its liquidity depth and institutional ownership.

**ETH (10%) — Small starter position**  
ETH is technically the weakest of the three right now. However, if/when it reclaims $1,800, there's a potential recovery back toward $2,500+. A small 10% position captures that optionality without significant downside exposure. Do not add to ETH until $1,800 is reclaimed on the weekly.
