## 💼 LinkedIn

Most governance systems have a free rider problem — you vote with nothing on the line, so signal quality suffers.

I've been building Caldera, a governance dapp on the Internet Computer, to fix that. The mechanic is simple: follow a Leader Neuron, then commit ICP behind your position (ADOPT or REJECT) on any active NNS proposal. If enough ICP stacks up on one side to clear the threshold, the Leader votes that way and the committed ICP is burned to cycles that fund the app itself. If the threshold isn't met, everyone gets refunded.

No free rides. Your capital is your conviction.

Beyond burn voting, Caldera also supports multi-token commits (ckBTC, ckETH, ckUSDC, ckUSDT with an internal swap to ICP), pooled staking for people who want to participate without giving up custody, and a community idea board for directing what gets built next. It's fully on-chain — sign in with Internet Identity, no accounts, no custodians.

Still early, but the live app is up and running. If you're building or participating in ICP governance, I'd love your feedback.

## 💬 Discord Forum Post

**Title:** Caldera — burn ICP to vote on NNS proposals, skin-in-the-game governance on ICP

Hey all — wanted to share something I've been heads-down building: **Caldera**, a governance dapp on the Internet Computer.

The core idea: instead of just following a neuron and rubber-stamping votes, you actually commit ICP behind your position on a proposal. If the committed ICP clears a threshold, the Leader Neuron votes the winning side and the ICP is burned to cycles that fund the canister. If it doesn't hit threshold, everyone gets their ICP back. Zero risk if the community doesn't agree.

What's live right now:
- Burn voting on active NNS proposals (weighted by ICP committed)
- Progressive onboarding — browse anon, sign in with Internet Identity, follow the neuron, then commit
- Multi-token support (ckBTC, ckETH, ckUSDC, ckUSDT — swapped to ICP internally)
- Pooled staking for participation without custody transfer
- Community idea board to vote on what gets built next

It's fully on-chain and self-sustaining — the more it's used, the longer it runs.

Repo: https://github.com/andreij6/proof-of-burn

Would love feedback — especially on the onboarding flow and whether the burn mechanic makes sense to people coming in fresh. What would make you actually use this over just setting your neuron to follow?
