# User & Stakeholder Overview

This document provides a high-level, non-technical explanation of the **Proof of Burn** application. It is designed for users, community members, and stakeholders to understand the purpose of the application, how it operates, and the benefits of this novel governance model.

---

## 1. What is "Proof of Burn"?

**Proof of Burn** is a decentralized governance platform built on the Internet Computer Protocol (ICP). Its core mission is to introduce **skin in the game** to liquid democracy. 

In traditional governance, users delegate their voting power to a "primary neuron" (the Leader Neuron) by selecting it as a followee. The Leader Neuron then votes on their behalf. However, there is often no clear mechanism for followers to communicate their level of conviction on specific proposals, nor is there a way to ensure the Leader Neuron only votes when the community truly cares.

Proof of Burn solves this by combining **neuron following** with **token burning**:
1. **Follow the Leader:** Users follow the primary Leader Neuron on the Network Nervous System (NNS).
2. **Commit & Vote Stance:** For any active NNS proposal, users can commit an amount of liquid ICP to be burned and choose their stance: **ADOPT** or **REJECT**. **Each user may commit to a proposal exactly once. Commitments are final and cannot be changed or undone.**
3. **The 1-Hour Cutoff:** Exactly **1 hour** before the proposal closes on the NNS, the app closes all new commitments to prepare the on-chain vote.
4. **Weighted Count & Voting:** 
   - If the combined commitments (Adopt + Reject) meet or exceed the proposal's minimum threshold, the Leader Neuron votes in accordance with the winning choice (the pot with the higher amount of committed ICP). **All committed ICP in both pots is permanently burned.**
   - If the threshold is not met, the Leader Neuron **abstains** from voting, and all committed ICP is refunded to the users.

## 2. Why Burn ICP? The Value Proposition

> [!TIP]
> Burning tokens is the ultimate signal of conviction. Unlike voting with staked tokens (which costs nothing and carries no risk), burning tokens is a permanent sacrifice. It ensures that only those with genuine conviction influence the outcome of key decisions.

* **Skin in the Game:** Traditional voting is cheap. Proof of Burn forces participants to evaluate proposals carefully because casting a vote has a real economic cost — and it cannot be reversed.
* **Aggregated Impact:** Small holders who follow the Leader Neuron can combine their voices. By committing even small amounts of ICP, they help trigger a massive consolidated vote.
* **Deflationary Tokenomics:** Every successful vote permanently destroys ICP, decreasing the circulating supply. This aligns the governance process with the economic health of the entire ICP network.
* **Trustless by Design:** The canister code is immutable after deployment. No administrator can change the rules, withdraw escrow funds, or alter vote outcomes. The code is the law.
* **Self-Funding Protocol:** A small flat protocol fee (0.005 ICP) is charged per commitment. Fees are collected into an on-chain treasury and automatically converted to cycles to keep the canister running indefinitely — no developer intervention required.

## 3. The Progressive Disclosure Experience

To prevent users from being overwhelmed by complex blockchain actions, the application uses a **Progressive Disclosure** model. The single-page application reveals more options and controls as the user progresses through different validation levels (Tiers).

### The Four User Tiers

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Tier 0: Anonymous Visitor                                   |
| - Read-only view of active proposals and Leader Neuron ID   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
                              |
             (Signs in via Internet Identity)
                              v
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Tier 1: Authenticated User                                  |
| - Unlocks vote history logs and proposal threshold progress |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
                              |
                 (Verify Following on-chain)
                              v
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Tier 2: Verified Follower                                   |
| - Unlocks "Commit to Burn" input fields                     |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
                              |
                   (Commits ICP to a proposal)
                              v
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Tier 3: Active Participant                                  |
| - Unlocks Personal Dashboard, status tracker, & share tools |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

* **Tier 0 — Anonymous Visitor (Browse):** A visitor who hasn't connected a wallet. They see the Leader Neuron ID (with a copy button), a list of active proposals, and a call-to-action to sign in. They cannot see personal stats or interact with the burn mechanism.
* **Tier 1 — Authenticated (View Details):** The user has logged in. They can now see proposal progress bars (how close they are to the burn threshold) and view the detailed vote history of the Leader Neuron.
* **Tier 2 — Verified Follower (Interact):** The system has verified on-chain that the user's neuron follows the Leader Neuron. The user can now commit ICP to be burned.
* **Tier 3 — Active Participant (Dashboard):** The user has committed ICP to at least one active proposal. A personal dashboard strip appears at the top of the page, showing their total commitments, historical burns, and proposal status.

## 4. Key User Flow

1. **Discover:** The user visits the app, copies the Leader Neuron ID, and configures their neuron in the NNS Dapp to follow it.
2. **Connect:** The user logs in to the Proof of Burn app using **Internet Identity** — the native ICP identity standard. No third-party wallet is required.
3. **Verify:** The app automatically checks that the user's neuron is following the Leader Neuron (via on-chain hotkey query).
4. **Evaluate:** The user reads and evaluates the active proposals.
5. **Commit:** The user commits an amount of ICP (up to their staked neuron size) to be burned, and selects their stance (ADOPT or REJECT). A flat protocol fee of **0.005 ICP** is charged at this step to fund canister operating costs. **This action is final — there is no undo and no second commitment on the same proposal.**
6. **Outcome:**
   - **Threshold Met:** The Leader Neuron votes with the winning pot (Adopt or Reject) and all committed ICP is burned.
   - **Threshold Fails:** The Leader Neuron abstains, and all committed ICP is refunded to the users.
