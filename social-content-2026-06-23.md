## 💼 LinkedIn

**What if your vote cost you something?**

Most governance systems suffer from low-signal participation — people vote without consequence, which means noise drowns out conviction.

Caldera takes a different approach. It's a governance dapp built on the Internet Computer where you *commit* ICP to signal how strongly you believe in a proposal outcome (ADOPT or REJECT). If enough participants commit and the community clears the threshold, the Leader Neuron votes the winning side — and the committed ICP is burned to cycles that fund the app itself. If the threshold isn't met, everyone gets refunded.

No free votes. No consequence-free signaling. Just skin in the game.

Beyond the core burn voting mechanic, Caldera includes multi-token commits (ckBTC, ckETH, ckUSDC, ckUSDT), pooled staking, a community Idea Board, and it's fully self-sustaining — the more it's used, the longer it runs.

Everything runs fully on-chain. Sign in with Internet Identity and try it:

Open source: https://github.com/andreij6/proof-of-burn

## 💬 Discord Forum Post

**Title:** Caldera — burn ICP to vote on NNS proposals, fully on-chain

Hey everyone 👋

Been building a governance dapp on ICP called **Caldera** and wanted to share it here and get some feedback.

**The core idea:** you follow a Leader Neuron, then *commit* ICP behind ADOPT or REJECT on any active NNS proposal. Think of it as putting your money where your mouth is — if enough people commit and the threshold clears, the Leader votes the winning side and committed ICP gets burned to cycles that fund the canister. If the threshold isn't met, everyone gets refunded.

The interesting design constraint: the app is self-sustaining. Burned ICP → cycles → canister runs longer. More participation = longer runway. No external funding needed.

There's a bunch of other stuff in there too — multi-token commits (ckBTC/ckETH/ckUSDC/ckUSDT with an internal swap desk), pooled staking, a lossless lottery, a community Idea Board for suggesting what to build next, and some on-chain mini games (Mini Golf with a course editor, Field Goal).
